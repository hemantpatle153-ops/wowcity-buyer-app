**Full description:**
WowCity shows you clothes that are in stock right now at shops near you, so you know before you go.

FIND IT NEARBY
• See kurtas, sarees, jeans, shirts, kids wear and more from local shops around you
• Sort by nearest, newest or price
• Filter by category, brand, size, colour and price

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
• Price, sizes and colours in stock at each shop
• How far the shop is, with directions in Google Maps
• Shop address and phone when the shop shares them

SAVE YOUR FAVOURITES
• Save products you like and come back to them any time
• Browse without an account. Sign in with a one-time code only if you want to save favourites

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your location is used only to show what's near you, and only while you use the app
• No ads. We don't sell your data
• Delete your account in the app whenever you like
